# AU-STELLAR LIFE — Phase 1 Content Architecture

**Status:** Foundation approved for implementation planning

**Purpose:** Define how the master framework, coaching curriculum, assessments, membership tiers, and protected content fit together before building new pages or a paywall.

## 1. Product model

AU-STELLAR LIFE is organized as one transformation platform with three coaching pathways:

1. **AU-STELLAR INDIVIDUAL — Refine Yourself**
2. **AU-STELLAR RELATIONSHIP — Build Together**
3. **AU-STELLAR COMMUNITY — Strengthen Culture**

All three pathways use the same A.U.-S.T.E.L.L.A.R. master framework. The framework supplies the method for growth; the pathways identify where that method is applied.

```text
A.U.-S.T.E.L.L.A.R. Master Framework
├── Individual application
├── Relationship application
└── Community application
```

The framework is a recurring transformation cycle, not a rigid course that a person completes once. A member can revisit any principle as new challenges emerge.

## 2. Master framework

Each principle must have a distinct job. Together, the principles move a person from recognizing reality to creating durable impact.

### A — Awareness

**Role:** Observe present reality clearly.

**Definition:** Notice internal and external reality before attempting to change it.

**Topics:** Self-awareness, identity, belief systems, bias, emotional awareness, blind spots, and personal narrative.

**Essential question:** “What story am I telling myself that may not be completely true?”

**Distinct purpose:** Awareness identifies what is happening. It does not yet explain the cause or prescribe a solution.

### U — Understanding

**Role:** Interpret causes, influences, and repeated patterns.

**Definition:** Explore why a person, relationship, or community thinks, feels, and behaves as it does.

**Topics:** Trauma, attachment, personality, values, needs, psychology, neuroscience, and spiritual worldview.

**Essential question:** “Why do I keep repeating this pattern?”

**Distinct purpose:** Understanding explains what Awareness reveals. Explanation should create responsibility and choice, not excuse harmful behavior.

### S — Strategy

**Role:** Design an intentional path forward.

**Definition:** Create practical systems that replace destructive patterns and support desired outcomes.

**Topics:** Habits, communication, boundaries, goal setting, decision-making, and conflict resolution.

**Essential question:** “What practical system will move me toward the person I want to become?”

**Distinct purpose:** Strategy converts insight into a plan with observable actions.

### T — Transformation

**Role:** Practice change until it becomes embodied.

**Definition:** Use consistent action, reflection, and correction to produce lasting change.

**Topics:** Accountability, discipline, emotional regulation, practice, reflection, and feedback.

**Essential question:** “What action can I take today?”

**Distinct purpose:** Transformation is the execution and integration of Strategy, not simply the desire to change.

### E — Excellence

**Role:** Continually refine quality and character.

**Definition:** Pursue mature, skillful, and ethical growth without confusing excellence with perfection.

**Topics:** Character, integrity, leadership, humility, wisdom, and service.

**Essential question:** “How can I become 1% better today?”

**Distinct purpose:** Excellence establishes the standard and direction of refinement after change begins.

### L — Love

**Role:** Direct growth toward the good of people.

**Definition:** Express care through truthful, respectful, responsible, and intentional action.

**Topics:** Marriage, parenting, friendship, trust, forgiveness, and empathy.

**Essential question:** “How can I love this person well today?”

**Distinct purpose:** Love prevents growth and excellence from becoming self-centered. Love includes truth, boundaries, repair, and responsibility; it does not require tolerating abuse or enabling harm.

### L — Legacy

**Role:** Extend positive impact beyond the present moment.

**Definition:** Steward influence and resources in ways that strengthen future people and generations.

**Topics:** Mentorship, family, business, community, stewardship, and generosity.

**Essential question:** “What will remain because I lived?”

**Distinct purpose:** Legacy turns individual and relational growth into durable contribution.

### A — Alignment

**Role:** Bring inner commitments and outward behavior into agreement.

**Definition:** Regularly compare beliefs, values, purpose, and actions, then repair inconsistencies.

**Topics:** Purpose, calling, faith, vision, mission, and consistency.

**Essential question:** “Does my life reflect what I say I believe?”

**Distinct purpose:** Alignment is the framework’s integrity check. It is continuous rather than a one-time stage.

### R — Resilience

**Role:** Continue the cycle through adversity, failure, and change.

**Definition:** Adapt, recover, learn, and keep growing without allowing hardship to become the whole identity.

**Topics:** Suffering, hope, courage, adaptability, perseverance, and recovery.

**Essential question:** “How can this hardship refine me rather than define me?”

**Distinct purpose:** Resilience protects progress when circumstances disrupt the original plan and returns the person to renewed Awareness.

## 3. How the framework operates

The acronym supplies a memorable sequence, but several principles remain active throughout the entire process.

```text
Awareness → Understanding → Strategy → Transformation
      ↓             ↓             ↓             ↓
Excellence • Love • Legacy • Alignment • Resilience
      └────────────── renewed Awareness ──────────┘
```

- **Awareness, Understanding, Strategy, and Transformation** form the primary change process.
- **Excellence** supplies the standard of refinement.
- **Love** supplies the relational and ethical direction.
- **Legacy** supplies the long-term horizon.
- **Alignment** checks consistency between values and behavior.
- **Resilience** sustains and renews the process through adversity.

This interpretation preserves the full A.U.-S.T.E.L.L.A.R. identity without pretending that every life challenge unfolds in a perfectly linear order.

## 4. Coaching curriculum architecture

Every curriculum module uses the same learning structure:

1. **Orientation:** Why the subject matters.
2. **Awareness:** Identify present conditions and patterns.
3. **Understanding:** Explore causes and influences.
4. **Strategy:** Select tools, boundaries, habits, or systems.
5. **Transformation:** Practice the selected actions.
6. **Integration:** Apply Excellence, Love, Legacy, Alignment, and Resilience.
7. **Evidence:** Complete a reflection, exercise, assessment, or observable action.
8. **Review:** Evaluate progress and choose the next step.

Each module may contain:

- A teaching lesson
- Learning objectives
- Core ideas
- Reflection prompts
- Practical exercises
- Downloadable worksheets
- A short knowledge or self-reflection check
- Coaching discussion prompts
- An action plan
- A progress checkpoint

## 5. Membership and access model

Membership is cumulative:

| Tier | Name | Curriculum access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Individual | AU-STELLAR Individual |
| 2 | Relationship | Individual + Relationship |
| 3 | Community | Individual + Relationship + Community |

The access model should eventually support at least four roles:

| Role | Intended access |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor | Public marketing, framework overview, and free entry assessment |
| Member | Content allowed by the member’s paid tier |
| Coach | Assigned-client results and coaching tools, subject to consent and policy |
| Administrator | Content, account, access, and platform administration |

Role names describe the content rule. They do not create security by themselves. Authentication and server-side authorization must enforce these rules when the protected platform is built.

## 6. Public and protected content boundary

### Public content

The public website may show:

- The names and concise definitions of all nine framework principles
- Each essential framework question
- A limited selection of representative topics
- An overview of the three coaching pathways
- Curriculum domain names and high-level outcomes
- A useful free AU-STELLAR Life Assessment
- A summary of the assessment result
- Sample excerpts intentionally selected for marketing or education
- Membership descriptions and enrollment options

### Protected content

The member platform should protect:

- Complete lessons and teaching scripts
- Full framework instruction and examples
- Exercises and downloadable worksheets
- Detailed reflection sequences
- Answer histories and assessment results
- Personalized recommendations and action plans
- Progress tracking and reassessment comparisons
- Coaching prompts and session preparation
- Coach notes and client records
- AI coaching conversations grounded in member content
- Facilitator guides, certification rubrics, and professional resources

Protected content must not be stored in or delivered with the public React bundle. Hiding it with CSS, a disabled button, an unlinked route, or a client-side condition is not a paywall.

## 7. Assessment architecture

Assessments are coaching and self-reflection tools. They must not be presented as clinical diagnosis, mental-health treatment, or emergency support.

### Assessment 1 — AU-STELLAR Life Assessment

**Access:** Public entry assessment; authenticated users may save results later.

**Scored areas, 1–10:**

- Identity
- Purpose
- Emotional Health
- Physical Health
- Spiritual Life
- Relationships
- Marriage
- Parenting
- Friendships
- Career
- Finances
- Habits
- Discipline
- Leadership
- Community

**Public output:**

- AU-STELLAR Life Wheel
- Category scores
- Highest areas
- Growth opportunities
- Brief non-clinical interpretation
- Suggested starting pathway

The public result should not expose paid lessons. It may recommend relevant curriculum domains by name.

### Assessment 2 — Identity Assessment

**Access:** Tier 1 and above.

**Explores:** Identity, beliefs, fears, motivation, avoidance, and values.

### Assessment 3 — Relationship Assessment

**Access:** Tier 2 and above.

**Measures:** Trust, safety, respect, communication, conflict, affection, intimacy, and shared vision.

Because relationship answers can be highly sensitive, individual and shared results must remain separate unless a person explicitly chooses to share them.

### Assessment 4 — Purpose Assessment

**Access:** Tier 1 and above.

**Explores:** Strengths, skills, interests, experience, overcome pain, and problems the member feels called to solve.

### Assessment 5 — Character Assessment

**Access:** Tier 1 and above.

**Evaluates:** Integrity, humility, courage, responsibility, compassion, patience, self-control, and gratitude.

Results should support reflection and growth rather than label a person as having good or bad character.

### Assessment 6 — Growth Readiness Assessment

**Access:** A short version may be public; the complete version and saved results belong to Tier 1 and above.

**Identifies:** Motivation, commitment, time availability, willingness to change, obstacles, and support systems.

### Future Tier 3 assessment group

Tier 3 will eventually require assessments for leadership, culture, mentorship, organizational health, community impact, and legacy projects. These should be defined when the Community curriculum is developed in detail.

## 8. Assessment result model

Every protected assessment result should eventually connect to action rather than end with a number.

```text
Assessment response
    ↓
Category scores and written reflections
    ↓
Strengths and growth opportunities
    ↓
Recommended curriculum domains
    ↓
Worksheet or action plan
    ↓
Coaching discussion
    ↓
Progress checkpoint and reassessment
```

Scores should be treated as a snapshot based on self-reported answers, not as permanent facts about a person.

## 9. Content relationships

The complete platform relationship is:

```text
Assessment — identifies where someone is today
Framework — explains the shared method of growth
Curriculum — teaches the relevant concepts and skills
Worksheets — turn teaching into personal application
AI Coach — reinforces approved learning through conversation
Coaching Sessions — personalize interpretation and action
Community — supports practice and long-term growth
```

The framework is the common language connecting every part of the system.

## 10. Content naming and identifiers

Stable identifiers should be used in code and stored data so display names can evolve without breaking relationships.

Suggested patterns:

```text
framework.awareness
framework.understanding
framework.strategy

individual.identity
individual.emotional-regulation

relationship.communication
relationship.trust

community.mentorship
community.legacy-projects

assessment.life
assessment.identity
assessment.relationship
```

Every future lesson should identify:

- Its pathway
- Its curriculum domain
- The framework principles it applies
- Its required membership tier
- Its version and publication status

This makes it possible to recommend content from assessment results without hard-coding page links throughout the application.

## 11. Content quality rules

Before a principle, module, assessment, or worksheet is published, verify that it:

1. Serves the AU-STELLAR LIFE mission rather than merely filling the acronym.
2. Has a distinct purpose and avoids unnecessary duplication.
3. Can be explained in plain language.
4. Produces a practical reflection, decision, or action.
5. Avoids unsupported clinical claims.
6. Handles trauma, relationships, faith, and sensitive data responsibly.
7. Clearly distinguishes education and coaching from licensed healthcare, legal, or financial advice.
8. Can be revised without breaking the larger framework.

## 12. Phase 1 decisions and open editorial questions

### Decisions established

- A.U.-S.T.E.L.L.A.R. is the master framework for all programs.
- The framework is reusable and cyclical rather than a one-time linear course.
- Individual, Relationship, and Community are application pathways.
- Membership access is cumulative.
- The Life Assessment is the public entry point.
- Detailed instruction, results, worksheets, and coaching data are protected.
- Server-side authorization will be required for the real paywall.
- Assessment scores support coaching reflection and do not represent clinical diagnosis.

### Questions to test during curriculum writing

- Does every framework principle remain distinct when applied to real lessons?
- Does Excellence describe an ongoing standard clearly enough to differ from Transformation?
- Does Alignment work best as a continuous integrity check rather than a sequential stage?
- Does Legacy need different applications for individuals, families, businesses, and communities?
- Which Life Assessment categories should be optional when they do not apply to a participant?
- What language and escalation guidance will be used when answers indicate abuse, self-harm, or another crisis?
- Which content is educational, which is coaching material, and which requires review by a licensed subject-matter professional?

These questions do not block the architecture. They are quality checks that should remain active as the content develops.
